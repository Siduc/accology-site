CIS subcontractor refunds

If a contractor has taken CIS tax off your pay, you may be due some of it back.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

The Construction Industry Scheme is the system where a contractor deducts tax before paying a subcontractor. Too much can be taken, or expenses and allowances can mean a repayment is due. Some people claim during the year. Others wait for self assessment. We will tell you which applies.

Where the claim is handled

CIS refund enquiries are taken on a separate site: cisrefunds.online.

Go to cisrefunds.online

What you can send

  • Payment and deduction statements from contractors
  • Photos of invoices and expenses
  • A forwarded email from the contractor or from HMRC

Fee

There is a draft guide fee on the pricing page. It is not a quote. Whether that monthly fee includes a self assessment return is still to be confirmed, on the same page.
