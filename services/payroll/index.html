Payroll

We run the payroll from what you tell us. You check the figures before they are filed.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

What you tell us

  • Who is being paid, and the hours or the salary
  • Starters, leavers and any change of pay
  • Statutory pay, if someone is off sick or on family leave, once you know the dates

An email is enough. You do not need payroll software.

What we do

  • Calculate the pay and the deductions
  • Send you the figures to approve
  • Issue the payslips
  • Send the payroll report HMRC requires on or before payday

We do not give employment-law advice. We run the payroll from the facts you give us.
