VAT returns

We prepare your VAT return from the invoices and statements you send, and we file it with HMRC.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

This is VAT, not Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. If you are a sole trader or a landlord wondering about the 2026 income-tax rules, that is the Making Tax Digital for Income Tax page.

What you can send

  • Sales and purchase invoices, as photos or PDFs
  • Bank statements
  • Emails from suppliers and from HMRC, forwarded as they arrive

What we do

  • Prepare the return for the periods HMRC has set
  • Keep the digital VAT records that Making Tax Digital for VAT requires, from what you send
  • File the return with software that works with HMRC, so you do not have to run that software yourself

Tell us which VAT scheme you are on. If you are not sure, we will check. Almost all VAT-registered businesses have to keep digital VAT records. A few are exempt. Say if you think that includes you.

Usual deadline

Many businesses file quarterly. If you pay electronically, the return and the payment are often due one calendar month and seven days after the period ends. We use the dates on your HMRC account.

These are the usual deadlines. Yours can differ, and we will confirm the dates that apply to you.
