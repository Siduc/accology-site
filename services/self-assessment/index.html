Self assessment

Personal tax returns for sole traders, landlords and company directors.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

Who this is for

  • Sole traders, including people in their first year of trade
  • Landlords with UK property income
  • Directors who need a return for salary, dividends, benefits, or other personal income

A director is not in self assessment only because they are a director. Many are, because of dividends or other income. We will tell you if a return is needed.

What you can send

  • Photos of expenses, or a simple list in an email
  • Bank statements for the business or the property
  • P60s, P11Ds, dividend paperwork and rental statements, forwarded as they are

What we do

  • Prepare the return and the extra pages that apply (self-employment, UK property, employment)
  • File it with HMRC online
  • Tell you the tax and when it is due, including payments on account if they apply

Payments on account, where they apply, are usually due on 31 January and 31 July. We will say if that is you, before the date arrives.

Usual deadlines

  • If you have not filed before, HMRC needs to know by 5 October after the end of the tax year. Tell us earlier than that.
  • Online returns are due by 31 January after the end of the tax year. We file online.
  • The balancing payment is usually due on the same 31 January.

These are the usual deadlines. Yours can differ, and we will confirm the dates that apply to you.

Many sole traders and landlords also have to follow Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. The year-end return still exists alongside it. See Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.
