Confirmation statements and company secretarial

We file the confirmation statement, and we can deal with the routine company updates that go to Companies House.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

Confirmation statement

Every UK company has to file a confirmation statement at least once a year, even when nothing has changed. It checks the people, the shares and the registered details Companies House already holds.

You usually have 14 days after the end of the 12-month review period to file it. It can be filed earlier. Companies House charges a fee to file it.

These are the usual deadlines. Yours can differ, and we will confirm the dates that apply to you.

Other company paperwork

We can also help with:

  • Appointing or removing a director
  • Changing a registered office or a director's service address
  • Updating people with significant control
  • Issuing shares, where we have agreed to do that work
