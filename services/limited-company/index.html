Limited company accounts and corporation tax

We prepare your company's accounts and its corporation tax return from the papers you already have.

Request a consultation

You can send the information in the simplest form you have: a photo, a PDF statement, or an email forwarded to us. You do not need accounting software.

What you can send

  • Photos of receipts and invoices
  • Bank statements, as a PDF or a photo
  • Emails forwarded to us, including ones from the bank or from a supplier
  • Last year's accounts, if you have them, and any letter from HMRC or Companies House

What we do

  • Write up the records from what you send
  • Prepare the annual accounts
  • Prepare the corporation tax return (the CT600) and the tax computation
  • File the accounts at Companies House and the return with HMRC
  • Tell you, in plain language, if something is still missing

If the company qualifies for a simpler filing, we use it, and we tell you which one applies.

Usual deadlines

  • Most private companies file accounts at Companies House within nine months of the year end.
  • Most small companies pay corporation tax nine months and one day after the end of the accounting period. A large company can be on a different timetable.
  • The company tax return is due twelve months after the end of the accounting period.

These are the usual deadlines. Yours can differ, and we will confirm the dates that apply to you.

Fee

There is a draft starting fee for this work on the pricing page. It is not a quote. We agree the fee in writing before we start.

That figure is for the company. It does not include a director's own self assessment, VAT, payroll, or the confirmation statement, unless we have agreed that in writing.
